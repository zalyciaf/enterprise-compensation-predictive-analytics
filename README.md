# Enterprise Compensation Predictive Analytics

## Overview

This project analyzes global data science compensation trends from 2020–2024 using exploratory data analysis, data visualization, and Multiple Linear Regression in R.

The primary objective is to identify how factors such as experience level, company size, employment type, job role, and reporting year influence compensation within the data science profession.

This project was developed for DA 6233: Data Analytics Visualization and Communication.

---

## Research Question

How do experience level, company size, employment type, job role, and reporting year influence data science salaries, and does the salary impact of experience level vary depending on company size?

---

## Objectives

- Analyze salary trends in the data science industry.
- Explore relationships between compensation and key workforce characteristics.
- Develop and evaluate Multiple Linear Regression models.
- Investigate interaction effects between experience level and company size.
- Communicate findings through visualizations and statistical interpretation.

---

## Dataset

**Source:** Kaggle

**Dataset:** Latest Data Science Salaries Dataset (2020–2024)

The dataset contains global compensation records for data science and analytics professionals, including information on:

- Salary in USD
- Experience Level
- Company Size
- Employment Type
- Job Title
- Company Location
- Employee Residence
- Reporting Year
